# Talent Acquisition Value Engine

**Engine Type:** Growth (Engineering Department)  
**Purpose:** To transform a hiring need into a fully productive engineer.

---

## Boundaries

| Element | Definition |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Triggering Event** | Hiring need identified or candidate applies |
| **Ending Event** | New dev completes first sprint and is fully productive |

---

## 🎯 Power Stages

| Power Stage | Why It's Critical | Playbook Opportunity |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Define Role & Requirements** | Without crystal-clear requirements and leveling criteria, you'll waste weeks interviewing mismatched candidates and create misaligned expectations for the team. A bad job description attracts the wrong talent and repels the right talent. | **Role Definition Playbook**: Template-driven job description builder with must-have vs. nice-to-have framework, seniority leveling matrix, and compensation band guidelines. |
| **Technical Assessment & Team Interviews** | This is where most hiring decisions are made—and most mistakes happen. Without structured rubrics and consistent evaluation criteria, you introduce bias, miss red flags, and lose great candidates to inconsistent experiences. | **Interview & Assessment Playbook**: Standardized technical assessment designs with scoring rubrics, behavioral interview question bank aligned to engineering values, and debrief facilitation guide. |
| **Day 1 Orientation & Environment Setup** | The first 48 hours determine whether a new engineer feels empowered or lost. A smooth setup gets them coding fast; a broken one destroys confidence and costs weeks of productivity. This is where onboarding is won or lost. | **Day 1 Launch Playbook**: Pre-start checklist for accounts/access, buddy assignment protocol, environment setup validation checklist, and first-task selection criteria. |
