# 📝 Workplace Improvement Program (WIP) Implementation Playbook

## DEFINE
| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Playbook Name | "Workplace Improvement Program (WIP): Request, Approval, and Fund Governance" |
| Purpose | To standardize how team members request workplace hardware/gear, how approvers evaluate requests, and how HR administers WIP funds using a needs-based, ROI-first framework. |
| Owner | HR / Operations |
| Last Updated | 2026-04-27 |
| Review Cycle | Every 90 days |

## Prerequisites
Before starting, ensure the following are in place:

- [ ] Team member understands WIP is **needs-based** (not automatic upgrades)  
        
- [ ] Requestor has identified the exact item and estimated cost  
        
- [ ] Requestor has checked WIP fund availability with HR (fund formula remains internal)  
        
- [ ] Team Lead understands item tier rules (Green / Yellow / Red / Purple)  
        
- [ ] Approvers understand escalation rules for requests above Php 10,000 and all overdrafts  
        
- [ ] Team Lead has access to the official [HR request form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdBPcmA5dXdOHMil4KKauS_lWoyxdv1Ll1nJt8p2s_C24T6wA/viewform)

- [ ] Employee acknowledges ownership/lifecycle and reimbursement requirements (including invoice submission)

## Step-by-Step Checklist
### Phase 1: Identify and Validate Need

1. **Confirm request is operationally necessary** (not preference-only upgrade).  
2. **Capture required request details**:  
- Item name  
- Estimated cost (Php)  
- Business impact / need statement  
- Justification (mandatory for 🟨 Yellow items)  
3. **Classify the request by tier**:  
- 🟩 **Green (Essential):** Mic, Speakers, Headset, UPS  
- 🟨 **Yellow (Conditional):** Solar Generator, Gas Generator, Monitor (requires strong use-case)  
- 🟥 **Red (Pipeline):** Not currently approved; logged for policy review  
- 🟪 **Purple (Luxury/Optional):** Chair, Table (allowed only after baseline essentials are met)

---

### Phase 2: Route Request by Role

4. **If requestor is an Individual Contributor (Member):** submit to Team Lead.  
5. **If requestor is a Team Lead requesting for self:** submit to Direct Supervisor (Department Head).  
6. **Use a complete request format** to avoid back-and-forth delays.

---

### Phase 3: Team Lead / Supervisor Evaluation

7. **Evaluate against needs-based policy** (missing essentials first).  
8. **Validate tier eligibility** and required justifications.  
9. **Check cost reasonableness** and expected operational value.  
10. **For 🟥 Red items:** do not approve; add to policy pipeline.  
11. **For 🟪 Purple items:** confirm all baseline essentials are already fulfilled.

**SLA (Approval Timelines):**

- Team Lead decision: **maximum 1 week**  
- HR decision after endorsed submission: **maximum 1 week**

---

### Phase 4: Fund, Threshold, and Overdraft Validation

12. **Validate fund availability** via HR records (formula not publicly disclosed).  
13. **Budget threshold rule:** requests above **Php 10,000** require **Department Head \+ HRBP** approval.  
14. **Overdraft rule:** if critical need exceeds available fund, overdraft may be considered.  
15. **All overdrafts require mandatory approval from Department Head and HR.**  
16. **If overdraft approved:**  
- Balance goes negative  
- Employee cannot submit new requests until balance is positive again via accrual  
17. **Accrual cap remains:** maximum **Php 30,000**.

---

### Phase 5: Formal Submission to HR

18. Once approved at required level(s), submit through the official form:  
    [HR WIP Request Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdBPcmA5dXdOHMil4KKauS_lWoyxdv1Ll1nJt8p2s_C24T6wA/viewform)  
19. Submission must include:  
- Employee name / role / team  
- Item \+ tier  
- Cost  
- Justification  
- Fund/overdraft status  
- Required approvers (including threshold/overdraft approvers where applicable)

---

### Phase 6: Procurement and Reimbursement Rules

20. **No preferred vendor list** is enforced.  
21. **Reimbursement is capped** at employee’s allotted funds unless overdraft is pre-approved.  
22. **Invoices are mandatory**; without invoice compliance, no subsequent request can proceed.  
23. **Brand/model options may be proposed** by approvers/HR; employee proposals for preferred items will be considered.

---

### Phase 7: Asset Lifecycle, Replacement, and Accountability

24. **Replacement cycle:** 2 years.  
25. **Ownership policy:**  
- Company Property (returnable upon exit), or  
- Employee Property after 24 months (per policy conditions)  
26. **Misused/Lost/Damaged items** count against employee’s accumulated funds.

---

### Phase 8: Tracking, Appeals, and Governance

27. **System of record:** HR Spreadsheet (fund balances, approvals, issuance, lifecycle).  
28. **Appeals path:** employee submits a necessity proposal.  
29. **Appeal escalation level:** one level above direct supervisor.  
30. **Appeal SLA:** maximum **1 week** for decision.

## Common Mistakes & Fixes
| Mistake | Fix |
| :---- | :---- |
| Submitting vague requests | Require item, cost, impact, and clear justification |
| Delayed approvals | Enforce 1-week Team Lead and 1-week HR SLAs |
| Missing higher-level approval on large requests | Auto-route \> Php 10,000 to Department Head \+ HRBP |
| Treating overdraft as optional shortcut | Require Department Head \+ HR approval for all overdrafts |
| Filing new requests without invoice compliance | Block new request until invoice is submitted |
| Assuming only HR-selected brands are allowed | Allow employee-proposed brand/model for consideration |
| Not tracking asset incidents | Log misuse/loss/damage and deduct from accumulated funds |
| Inconsistent appeal handling | Standardize one-level-higher escalation and 1-week SLA |

## Success Criteria
This playbook is complete when:

- [ ] Requests follow correct routing (Member vs Team Lead self-request)  
- [ ] Team Lead and HR approvals are completed within 1 week each  
- [ ] Requests above Php 10,000 include Department Head \+ HRBP approval  
- [ ] All overdrafts include Department Head \+ HR approval  
- [ ] Reimbursements and invoices are processed per policy  
- [ ] Asset lifecycle and incident accountability are recorded  
- [ ] Appeals are escalated correctly and resolved within 1 week  
- [ ] All records are updated in HR Spreadsheet

## Standards & Governance
- **Needs-based priority** is mandatory across all teams.  
- **Funding formula is internal** and not disclosed in SOP-facing documentation.  
- **Approvals must follow SLA** and authority thresholds.  
- **All overdrafts require explicit dual approval** (Department Head \+ HR).  
- **Documentation compliance (invoice \+ tracker logging)** is required for continuity.  
- **Asset accountability** is enforced through fund impact for misuse/loss/damage.
