# 🌟 AI Fun Share Prototype Exemption Playbook

## DEFINE
| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Playbook Name | AI Fun Share Prototype Exemption Playbook |
| Purpose | Define how an engineer earns a 4-week exemption from the weekly #ai-fun-share participation requirement by getting a prototype plan pre-approved, building and posting the prototype, and receiving a :star: reaction from the primary reviewer. |
| Owner | Alan Baldelobar |
| Last Updated | 2026-09-30 |
| Review Cycle | Every 90 days |

## Prerequisites
Before starting, ensure you have:

- [ ] An idea for an AI prototype you want to build (tool, dashboard, automation, analysis, etc.)
- [ ] A written **prototype plan** describing what you will build, why, and the expected benefit
- [ ] Access to your **direct supervisor** for plan approval
- [ ] Membership and posting access in the `#ai-fun-share` Slack channel
- [ ] Understanding that the standard weekly #ai-fun-share posting requirement **always applies unless an active exemption is in effect** (see Step-by-Step Checklist)
- [ ] A way to deliver the finished prototype for review (link, branch, dashboard URL, or equivalent)

---

## Step-by-Step Checklist

### Phase 1: Prepare the Prototype Plan

1. **Draft a written prototype plan** — what you will build, the problem it solves, the tools/models involved, and the expected outcome.
2. **Keep it concise** — the plan only needs enough detail for your supervisor to judge whether the prototype is worth the time.
3. **Do not start building yet** — building before approval does not count toward an exemption and wastes effort if the plan is not approved.

---

### Phase 2: Get the Plan Approved (1 week)

4. **Submit the plan to your direct supervisor** for approval.
   - The approval process takes **up to one week** between the supervisor and you.
5. **IF the supervisor is confident, THEN they approve the plan** and you may proceed to Phase 3.
6. **IF the supervisor is unsure about any part of the plan, THEN escalate it to the primary reviewer (Alan)** for a final call — this step is optional and only needed when the supervisor is not confident.
   - The supervisor approval is the **primary** gate; reviewer sign-off is a fallback, not a replacement.
7. **IF the plan is NOT approved, THEN proceed as normal**:
   - Keep posting the standard weekly #ai-fun-share.
   - You get **no exemption** and may retry with a revised plan later.
8. **During the entire approval week, still submit a normal #ai-fun-share** — the participation requirement is not paused while approval is pending.

---

### Phase 3: Build the Approved Prototype

9. **Build the prototype** once approved.
10. **Continue submitting the weekly #ai-fun-share** while you build — do not stop posting just because a plan is in flight or approved.
11. **Complete the prototype** and hand it to your direct supervisor for review.

---

### Phase 4: Post and Earn the Exemption

12. **Post the finished prototype** to `#ai-fun-share` (or as instructed) so the primary reviewer can react to it.
13. **Receive a :star: reaction from the primary reviewer** on a top-level post.
    - :star: is given **only** to approved prototype posts, never to ordinary weekly shares.
14. **Exemption takes effect** once the :star: is placed:
    - The author is exempt from the #ai-fun-share participation condition for the **post's week plus the next three weeks (4 weeks total)**.
    - Example: a prototype posted the week of Sep 13–19 exempts the author through Oct 4–10.
15. **Keep working normally** during the exemption — the exemption covers the *reporting* condition only; it does not reduce expected engineering output.

---

### Phase 5: Maintain Cadence Across Cycles

16. **First prototype cycle**: you must post **both** the prototype **and** the standard weekly #ai-fun-share — an exemption has not yet been earned.
17. **Later cycles — string approvals inside active exemption time**:
    - While an existing 4-week exemption is still active, submit your **next** prototype plan for approval.
    - Because approval takes up to one week, a plan submitted early in a current exemption window is approved **before** the exemption lapses.
    - On the next approval week you therefore **no longer need to create a fresh #ai-fun-share** — the existing exemption covers it.
18. **IF the exemption lapses with no approved prototype posted, THEN resume standard weekly shares** on the following week.

## Common Mistakes & Fixes

| Common Mistake | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Building a prototype before the plan is approved | Get written supervisor approval **first**; unapproved builds earn no exemption |
| Stopping weekly #ai-fun-share during the approval week | The participation requirement stays active while approval is pending — keep posting |
| Stopping weekly #ai-fun-share while building an approved prototype | Keep posting until the prototype is actually posted and the :star: is placed |
| Assuming supervisor approval alone grants the exemption | The exemption only starts after the prototype is **posted** and receives the **:star:** reaction |
| Escalating every plan to the reviewer | Escalate only when the supervisor is unsure; supervisor approval is the primary gate |
| Expecting the exemption to last longer than 4 weeks | The exemption covers exactly the post's week plus three more (4 total); re-earn by posting another approved prototype before it lapses |
| Counting an ordinary weekly share as a prototype | :star: is reserved for **approved prototype posts**, not routine shares |

## Success Criteria

- [ ] Prototype plan was approved by the direct supervisor (reviewer escalation used only when the supervisor was unsure)
- [ ] Standard weekly #ai-fun-share continued through the approval week and the build phase
- [ ] Finished prototype was submitted to the supervisor and posted
- [ ] Prototype post received a :star: from the primary reviewer
- [ ] Author shows as CLEAR on the #ai-fun-share participation condition for 4 weeks
- [ ] First-cycle: both prototype and standard share were posted
- [ ] Later-cycle: next plan was submitted inside the active exemption window, so no fresh share was needed on the next approval week
