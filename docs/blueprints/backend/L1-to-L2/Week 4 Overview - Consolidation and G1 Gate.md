Week 4 locks in the month's core achievement: a third independent decomposition, all three submitted to the Senior reviewer, and a Month 1 audit against the promotion gate document.

## What to Expect This Week

- **Day 1:** Independent decomposition #2 — a moderately complex brief (cross-cutting, spans multiple domains); complete all five sections, no guidance.
- **Day 2:** Independent decomposition #3 — a brief that requires a schema change and a nonzero migration effort; complete and document the decomposition.
- **Day 3:** Rework day. Revise decompositions #2–#3 against reviewer feedback; measure estimation accuracy against actuals from recent work.
- **Day 4:** Month 1 audit. Review all three decompositions plus Week 2's delivered feature against the gate matrix in the promotion gate document. Log a self-assessment against G1 (and G3 so far).
- **Day 5:** Skill-polish day (any weak area) + submit the complete G1 evidence set to the Senior reviewer.

## Week 4 Success Criteria

- [ ] 3 independent feature decompositions authored and documented (G1 hard bar in progress)
- [ ] ≥2 accepted by the Senior without re-decomposition (target)
- [ ] Estimation accuracy trend recorded (G1 soft signal)
- [ ] Month 1 self-audit completed against the promotion gate document
- [ ] G1 evidence set (decompositions + feature) handed to the Senior reviewer

## Guiding Principle

Decomposition that a Senior no longer has to redo is the single clearest signal that the L1→L2 shift has begun.
