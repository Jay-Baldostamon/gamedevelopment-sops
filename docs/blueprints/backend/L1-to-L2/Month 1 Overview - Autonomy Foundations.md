Month 1 shifts the engineer from executing pre-defined tickets to **autonomously scoping and delivering a bounded problem**. By the end of the month they have three Senior-accepted feature decompositions and their first full-stack feature shipped end-to-end with tests.

## What to Expect This Month

- **Week 1 — Decomposition Independence (G1):** Take a product brief and produce the full technical breakdown: required schema changes, API route design, component tree, and state-management approach. Two guided decompositions, then one independent.
- **Week 2 — Scoped Full-Stack Sprint (G3):** Deliver one real feature end-to-end from a brief with its own test coverage, without step-by-step guidance. Days 9–10 are skill-polish days if the sprint lands early.
- **Week 3 — AI Velocity & Ownership (G3):** Use AI beyond autocomplete — scaffold component trees, generate test harnesses, prototype schema and migrations — while detecting and refactoring suboptimal AI output.
- **Week 4 — Consolidation & G1 Gate (G1):** Decompose a third feature independently, submit all three decompositions to the Senior reviewer, and complete the Month 1 audit against the promotion gate document.

## Month 1 Success Criteria

- [ ] ≥3 feature decompositions authored, documented, and accepted by a Senior (G1 hard bar, in progress)
- [ ] Decomposition format (schema changes, API design, component tree, state approach) demonstrated on every feature
- [ ] ≥1 feature delivered end-to-end with tests covering the primary path and ≥1 boundary/edge case (G3 feature #1)
- [ ] No delivered feature required re-decomposition by a Senior
- [ ] Cracked decomposition effort estimates improve week over week (G1 soft signal)

## Month 1 Quality Gate Standard

- Blocking:
  - Three decompositions accepted by a Senior (Day 20 checkpoint)
  - First full-stack feature merged with tests
- Non-blocking:
  - Optional additional AI prototyping beyond required outputs
  - Exploration of state-management libraries beyond the team's default

## Month 1 Operating Metrics

- Decomposition acceptance count and rework rate (G1)
- Features delivered end-to-end with test coverage (G3)
- Estimation accuracy trend for decomposition effort (G1 soft signal)
- Review red-line / rework count on delivered features (G3)

## Guiding Principle

The shift is from **"I was given a ticket"** to **"I scoped the problem, split it, and shipped it."** Three accepted decompositions plus one shipped feature is the proof.
