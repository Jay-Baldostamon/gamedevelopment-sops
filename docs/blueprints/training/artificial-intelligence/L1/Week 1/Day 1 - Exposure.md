## Unity Game-Development Assignment

**Project baseline:** Continue one desktop 2D action-platformer in C# using the current Unity LTS release. Keep the game, repository, and issue history continuous across days.

**Unity outcome for this day:** Use the AI chat exercises to explore the action-platformer concept, player fantasy, movement feel, hazards, and a realistic first playable room; focus on exploration, not code.

**Validation and scope:** Use the Unity Editor, Play Mode, Unity Test Framework, and a desktop build when relevant to the task. Inspect actual results locally and identify any unavailable checks or blockers. Preserve scene, prefab, and `.meta` references; keep changes within authorized task scope; never commit secrets, licensed private assets, or generated `Library`/`Temp` output. Use approved mock game data, not unrelated production systems. Actual PRs still require the appropriate authorization, CI checks, and independent human review. No training reports or supervisor sign-offs are required.

### Session: Preparation

#### Prerequisites

Before starting, ensure the Engineer has:

* Company email account access
* Gemini access: <https://gemini.google.com/app>

### Session: Core Execution

#### Step-by-Step Checklist

### Phase 1: Setup

1. Log in to Gemini using your **company email**:  
   <https://gemini.google.com/app>

### Phase 2: Core Exposure Task

1. Create **5 separate chat threads** in Gemini.
2. Each thread must have at least:
   * **5 prompts/messages from Engineer**
   * **5 responses from Gemini**
3. Explore the player fantasy, movement feel, hazards, collectibles, and the first playable room.
   The goal is comfort with AI conversation flow, not production-ready code.

### Phase 3: Self-Unblock

1. If blocked or waiting for next task, Engineer should continue momentum through Gemini:

* State current learning topic
* State completed steps
* Ask for next step

1. Example prompt:

Today I'm learning to interact with AI. I've started by doing 5 chat threads to familiarize myself with AI capabilities and tools. What's my next step towards learning to utilize AI?



### Optional Exploration

Explore other approved AI features if useful. Do not share secrets or private assets.

### Session: Quality and Reflection

#### Common Mistakes & Fixes

| **Mistake** | **Fix** |
| --- | --- |
| Accepting an AI suggestion without considering the game | Ask follow-up questions about constraints and player experience |
| Over-investing in one difficult tool | Take notes and continue breadth exploration across other tools |

#### Standards & Governance

* Day 1 prioritizes interaction volume and familiarity over prompt sophistication.
* Engineers are expected to maintain forward momentum (self-unblock behavior) when blocked.
