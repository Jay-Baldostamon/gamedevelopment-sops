# Week 20 Overview: Month 5 Consolidation and Continuous QA Improvement

Week 20 consolidates SimplaBots (`hurdman/simplabots`) Month 5 zero-tolerance outcomes and embeds continuous QA improvement as an ongoing operating discipline.

The objective is to certify that high-quality delivery is normal rather than exceptional, codify the definitions of done and release controls as a durable baseline, and leave the team with a living improvement loop for the path ahead.

## What to Expect This Week

### Days 96-100 - Month 5 Consolidation and Continuous QA Improvement

This week transitions from enforcement into sustained operating excellence.

Core execution focus:

- consolidate Week 17-19 zero-tolerance and pipeline controls into one operating baseline
- validate release-confidence, DoD, visual, and drift metrics on a stable trajectory
- establish a repeatable continuous-QA-improvement loop with owned actions
- assess and record the QA culture and trust maturity reached
- publish a Month 5 closeout and steady-state operating brief

SimplaBots readiness surfaces:

- gate stability and release-confidence trajectory across Month 5
- resolves and prevents systemic defect patterns
- Definition of Done adherence as the standing release standard
- handoff materials for whoever owns QA operations going forward

## Week 20 Success Criteria

By the end of Week 20, QA should have:

- [ ] A consolidated Month 5 operating baseline and SOP references
- [ ] Metrics showing stable or improving DoD, release-confidence, and drift trends
- [ ] A documented continuous-QA-improvement loop with owned actions
- [ ] A recorded culture and trust-maturity assessment
- [ ] An executive Month 5 closeout and steady-state brief
- [ ] Evidence that high-quality delivery is the normal operating state

## Guiding Principle

Maturity is not a finish line; it is a maintained standard.

Week 20 ensures SimplaBots holds the quality bar in place and keeps improving it, so the trust built over five months is preserved rather than assumed.
