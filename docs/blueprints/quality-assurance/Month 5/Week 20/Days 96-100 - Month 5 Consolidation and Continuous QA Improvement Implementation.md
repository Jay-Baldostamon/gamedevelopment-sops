# Days 96-100: Month 5 Consolidation and Continuous QA Improvement

Days 96-100 formalize Month 5 closeout for SimplaBots (`hurdman/simplabots`) and transition quality assurance from a graduated onboarding program into a durable, continuously improving operating discipline.

The execution goal is to consolidate zero-tolerance controls, confirm stable quality trends, and leave behind a repeatable improvement loop and clear ownership.

### Session: Day 96 - Consolidate the Month 5 Operating Baseline

Compile Month 5 controls into one baseline:

- Week 17 zero-tolerance Definition of Done and acceptance-criteria enforcement
- Week 18 visual, copy, and layout correctness enforcement
- Week 19 mature branch protection and low-flake CI enforcement
- linked SOP references, role ownership, and exception paths

Required output:

- one consolidated Month 5 operating baseline package

### Session: Day 97 - Validate Metrics and Signal Quality

Evaluate Month 5 performance:

- DoD adherence and override trends
- release-confidence score stability across recent releases
- visual/copy/layout defect and baseline-churn trends
- flake count, drift inventory, and gate signal quality

Decision objective:

- identify any control requiring refinement before steady-state adoption

### Session: Day 98 - Establish the Continuous Improvement Loop

Make QA self-sustaining:

- define a recurring cadence for reviewing quality metrics and signals
- keep an owned backlog of improvement actions with ETAs
- route recurring defect patterns to policy or training fixes
- ensure every improvement has a measured before/after outcome

Governance requirement:

- the improvement loop has explicit ownership and a standing review cadence

### Session: Day 99 - Assess Culture and Trust Maturity

Capture the human dimension of Month 5:

- record whether high-quality delivery now feels like the norm
- survey how the gate is perceived (high-signal vs. friction)
- summarize trust in green signals and release confidence across teams
- document successes and remaining friction for the roadmap

Required output:

- one culture and trust-maturity assessment

### Session: Day 100 - Publish Month 5 Closeout and Steady-State Brief

Finalize communication and handoff:

- summarize Month 5 maturity and quality outcomes achieved
- present metric trends, residual risks, and improvement backlog
- document steady-state operating ownership and cadence
- provide the go-forward recommendation and confidence level

## Deliverables and Milestones

By the end of Day 100, the trainee should have:

- one consolidated Month 5 operating baseline package
- one metrics and signal-quality validation report
- one documented continuous-QA-improvement loop with owned actions
- one culture and trust-maturity assessment
- one executive Month 5 closeout and steady-state brief

## Implementation Examples

Example Month 5 steady-state checks:

1. DoD adherence stable at the target threshold
2. release-confidence score consistently above the go threshold
3. flake and drift trends reduced to accepted levels
4. improvement backlog owned with visible ETAs

Example closeout brief fields:

- quality maturity status and confidence level
- top residual risks
- owner commitments and steady-state cadence
- go-forward recommendation for QA operations

- [ ] Continuous-QA-improvement loop established with owned actions
- [ ] Culture and trust-maturity assessment recorded
