# Week 14 Overview: AI Instruction Compliance and QA Gate Synchronization

Week 14 scales Week 13 governance by turning instruction compliance into an auditable QA operating system for SimplaBots (`hurdman/simplabots`) and related automation workflows.

The objective is to ensure AI contributors consistently follow release-quality directives, and that QA gates adapt quickly when instruction policies evolve.

## What to Expect This Week

### Days 66-70 - AI Instruction Compliance and QA Gate Synchronization

This week formalizes how policy changes propagate from instruction files into QA behavior.

Core execution focus:

- define instruction-to-gate synchronization ownership and cadence
- monitor compliance drift across AI-generated changes
- standardize evidence that policy checks are being applied correctly
- calibrate block/advisory decisions for instruction non-compliance
- preserve developer velocity by keeping policy feedback actionable

SimplaBots-aware focus areas:

- release-note/changelog policy adherence
- quality gate behavior after policy updates
- consistency between engineering instructions and QA enforcement logic

## Week 14 Success Criteria

By the end of Week 14, QA should have:

- [ ] A documented sync lifecycle from instruction update to gate behavior update
- [ ] Compliance monitoring views for AI-generated change activity
- [ ] Standardized remediation guidance for policy-check failures
- [ ] Severity-aligned rules for when non-compliance blocks release
- [ ] Weekly evidence that QA and instruction governance remain aligned

## Guiding Principle

Policy is only real when enforcement and feedback are synchronized.

Week 14 ensures SimplaBots can evolve AI instructions without creating a lag between governance intent and QA gate behavior.
