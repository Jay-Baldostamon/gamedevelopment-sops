# Days 76-80: Month 4 Consolidation and Month 5 Readiness Implementation

Days 76-80 formalize Month 4 closeout for SimplaBots (`hurdman/simplabots`) and certify QA operating readiness before Month 5's stricter quality enforcement stage.

The execution goal is to consolidate governance controls, verify operational performance trends, and enter Month 5 with explicit risk ownership and decision clarity.

### Session: Day 76 - Consolidate Governance Baseline

Compile Month 4 controls into one baseline:

- Week 13 changelog/release-note automation governance
- Week 14 instruction-compliance synchronization controls
- Week 15 drift detection and autonomous-adjustment controls
- linked SOP references and role ownership

Required output:

- one consolidated Month 4 governance baseline package

### Session: Day 77 - Validate Control Performance and Signal Quality

Evaluate recent operating performance:

- compliance pass-rate trends
- drift incident trend and closure velocity
- gate signal quality (true positives vs noise patterns)
- unresolved policy and coverage mismatch counts

Decision objective:

- identify controls that require refinement before Month 5 adoption

### Session: Day 78 - Finalize Month 5 Enforcement Calibration

Prepare stricter enforcement settings:

- define which advisory checks should become blocking
- define guardrails for emergency override and exception logging
- ensure severity model remains consistent and explainable
- document communication plan for engineering and product stakeholders

Required artifact:

- one Month 5 calibration proposal with rationale and rollout plan

### Session: Day 79 - Resolve or Own Residual High-Risk Items

Close or assign remaining high-risk gaps:

- resolve quick-win blockers
- assign owner and ETA for unresolved high-risk items
- record dependency blockers and fallback mitigation
- ensure leadership visibility for every unresolved critical risk

Governance requirement:

- no unresolved high-risk item lacks owner and ETA

### Session: Day 80 - Publish Month 4 Closeout and Month 5 Entry Brief

Finalize readiness communication:

- summarize governance maturity achieved in Month 4
- present performance trends and residual risks
- present Month 5 enforcement calibration recommendation
- provide clear Month 5 entry decision and confidence level

## Deliverables and Milestones

By the end of Day 80, the trainee should have:

- one consolidated Month 4 governance baseline package
- one performance validation report for compliance, drift, and signal quality
- one Month 5 enforcement calibration proposal
- one residual high-risk ownership register with ETAs
- one executive Month 4 closeout and Month 5 entry brief

## Implementation Examples

Example Month 5 readiness checks:

1. instruction compliance trend stable or improving
2. release-note drift trend reduced to accepted threshold
3. unresolved high-risk items fully owned
4. block/advisory policy documented and communicated

Example executive summary fields:

- readiness status (`ready`, `ready-with-conditions`, `not-ready`)
- top residual risks
- owner commitments
- go-forward enforcement recommendation
