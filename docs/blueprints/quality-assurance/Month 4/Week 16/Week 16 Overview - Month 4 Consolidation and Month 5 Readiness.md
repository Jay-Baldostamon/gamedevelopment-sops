# Week 16 Overview: Month 4 Consolidation and Month 5 Readiness

Week 16 consolidates SimplaBots (`hurdman/simplabots`) Month 4 automation governance outcomes and prepares QA operations for the stricter Month 5 quality standard.

The objective is to finalize dependable operating controls for AI-driven delivery so the team can enter Month 5 with high-trust gates, explicit Definition of Done alignment, and low release-drift risk.

## What to Expect This Week

### Days 76-80 - Month 4 Consolidation and Month 5 Readiness

This week transitions from adaptation into readiness certification.

- consolidate Week 13-15 governance controls into one operating baseline
- confirm instruction compliance and release-note sync stability
- verify drift prevention process performance under recent contribution patterns

- publish readiness statement and residual risk register

SimplaBots readiness surfaces:

- changelog/release-note policy adherence and traceability
- QA gate stability across core workflow coverage
- unresolved blocker inventory and owner commitments
- leadership visibility into release confidence trajectory

Week 16 ensures SimplaBots enters Month 5 with disciplined governance, measurable readiness, and clear ownership for remaining risks.
